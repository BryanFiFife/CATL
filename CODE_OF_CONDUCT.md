# CATL community code of conduct

CATL welcomes technical discussion, criticism, bug reports and security research from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and specific.
- Critique code, claims and evidence rather than people.
- Give maintainers and contributors enough information to reproduce a problem.
- Disclose security vulnerabilities through the private route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- Do not publish credentials, personal data, customer material or exploit details that put live systems at risk.
- Assume good faith while still demanding evidence for security claims.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, deliberate disruption, spam, impersonation and knowingly exposing another person's secrets or private data are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation where necessary to protect the community or project.

For conduct concerns, contact Caledapt through its established business contact channel.

This code of conduct applies to CATL's project spaces, including issues, pull requests and project discussions.
